How to Play
Before each game starts you have 10 seconds to bet on which square the losing king will be standing on when the game ends — by checkmate or by running out of time.
⏱️
BETTING PHASE
You have 10 seconds before each game to place a bet. Choose a bet mode, select squares on the board, enter an amount, and click Place Bet. Your bet is deducted from your balance immediately.
♟️
THE GAME
Two AI engines play bullet chess against each other — 30 seconds per side. You watch. The game ends by checkmate or when a side runs out of time.
🎯
WINNING
Your bet wins if the losing king is standing on one of your selected squares at the end of the game — regardless of whether the game ended by checkmate or timeout.
🤝
DRAWS
If the game ends in a draw or stalemate, there is no losing king — all bets are lost for that round.
🎯 1 square — if the losing king is on your square you win ×64 your bet
⊞ 2×2 block (4 squares) — if the losing king is in your block you win ×16 your bet
💸 Miss or draw — you lose your bet
